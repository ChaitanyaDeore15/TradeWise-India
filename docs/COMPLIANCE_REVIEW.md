# Indian Legal, SEBI Regulatory & Ethical Compliance Review

## 1. Regulatory Context
The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) enforces strict regulations governing investment advice, research analysis, and algorithmic trading:
- **SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013**
- **SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014**
- **SEBI Circulars on Algorithmic Trading & FinTech Interfaces**

---

## 2. Boundaries of TradeWise India
To operate responsibly and legally without requiring SEBI RIA/RA licensing:
1. **No Actionable Buy/Sell Calls**: The platform never generates personalized buy or sell recommendations.
2. **No Real-Money Brokerage Execution**: The platform operates strictly with simulated paper trading accounts.
3. **No Unsubstantiated AI Profit Claims**: The AI tutor does not predict tomorrow's stock price or claim predictive statistical edge.
4. **Transparent Historical Backtests**: All backtests report net returns after estimated Indian taxes (STT, GST) and state that historical backtests do not guarantee future profitability.
5. **Clear Disclaimers**: Displayed prominently across the header, footer, backtest screens, and paper trading modules.

---

## 3. SEBI Retail Derivatives Risk Notice
In line with SEBI's September 2024 consultation paper, TradeWise actively educates users on the empirical findings that:
- **93% of individual retail traders in equity F&O incurred net trading losses** across FY22-FY24.
- Derivatives are leveraged instruments designed for portfolio risk hedging, not retail speculation.
